Dealing With Roof Leak Water Damage in New Rochelle

The spot on your ceiling is seldom directly below the hole in the roof. A loose shingle, a failed flashing joint or an aging seal around a skylight lets rain in, and it travels across the roof deck and framing until it falls into the insulation. Often the attic has been damp for some time before any brown mark reaches the room below. Our team maps everything that got wet, then dries it.

Our trade is water damage restoration, and roofing is a separate one. We work on the interior, which means removing wet insulation, drying drywall and framing, dealing with mold and rebuilding the ceiling. Should the leak still be active, our crew can cover that section with a tarp for now, or time our work with the roofing contractor you pick so nothing gets sealed over an open leak.

First Five Minutes

Before We Arrive

  1. Shut Off Wet CircuitsIf water is dripping through a light fixture, a ceiling fan or near an outlet, switch off that circuit at the breaker and leave the fixture alone.
  2. Contain the WaterPut a pan or bucket beneath each drip, and shift rugs, electronics and furniture clear of the wet area. Lay towels or plastic sheeting down to protect the floor.
  3. Relieve a Ceiling BulgeIf the ceiling is holding a pocket of water, stand to one side and poke a small hole at the lowest point so it drains into a bucket.
  4. Leave the Roof AloneClimbing on slick shingles or crawling around a dark attic is not worth the risk. Note from inside where the water is coming in and leave the rest to professionals.
  5. Document What You SeeTake photos and video of the ceiling, the attic and any damaged belongings before you move or throw anything away, then call us to start drying.
Will Insurance Pay?

What About Insurance?

Interior damage from a sudden, accidental roof leak, like one caused by a fallen limb or wind-torn shingles, is often covered under a homeowners policy. Leaks blamed on age, wear or skipped maintenance are usually denied, and the roof repair itself may be handled differently from the interior. Photos and moisture readings from us go to the adjuster, though only your policy decides coverage, and your agent can explain it.

Cost

What Affects the Cost

Common Causes

What Causes It

  • Aging, cracked seals around a skylight frame that no longer keep water out.
  • A vent pipe boot whose rubber has split from long sun exposure.
  • Gaps at chimneys, vents and sidewalls where the flashing has separated or rusted through.
  • Holes and fractures left in the roof after a branch or hailstones hit it.
  • Wind-torn, curling or split shingles that leave the underlayment open to rain.
If It Is Left

What Happens if You Wait

Drywall Ceilings

A ceiling panel left damp will discolor, bow and weaken. Joints open, tape peels, and a waterlogged piece may fall without much notice.

Attic Insulation

Once soaked, insulation flattens, loses its value and keeps the drywall under it damp for days. Fiberglass can sometimes be dried. Soaked cellulose usually has to be removed.

Wall Cavities

Rain sometimes follows the framing into a wall, where the insulation and the hidden side of the drywall take it up. Often the one clue is paint that blisters or trim that swells at the floor.

Mold in the Attic

A dark, warm attic with damp wood and paper-faced drywall suits mold well. Within 24 to 48 hours it can start growing, then move across the bottom of the roof deck.

Our Process

What Happens After You Call

1

Map Where Water Went

We inspect the attic, ceilings and wall cavities with moisture meters and thermal imaging to map everywhere the rain traveled, including areas that still look dry from the room below.

2

Tarp and Protect

When rain can still get in, a temporary tarp from us or a call to your roofer comes first. Anything sitting beneath the damage, including the flooring, gets protected too.

3

Tear Out and Dry

After removing the insulation and drywall that are past saving, our crew dries the attic and lower rooms with air movers and dehumidifiers and tracks moisture levels until the wood is dry.

4

Treat and Rebuild

Where it is called for, we apply an antimicrobial to the bare framing. Once the roof repair is done, we replace insulation, hang and finish new drywall, then prime and paint to match the ceiling.
